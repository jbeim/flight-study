N41533 DETECTION STUDY APP, VERSION 2.8.0: RESEARCHER QUICK GUIDE
(13 September 2026, evening)

WHAT CHANGED IN 2.8.0 (engine sound, the cockpit staying put, nose calibration,
picture steadying)

- ENGINE SOUND: WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG. 2.7.0 said the sound was fixed, and it
  was not. The sound check on the setup screen worked, and inside the flight the
  soundtrack really was playing, but at volume zero. The soundtrack fades in over
  0.4 s so there is no click when a run starts part-way into the file, and that
  fade was driven by the page's animation clock. Inside VR the headset stops that
  clock (only the VR frame loop runs), so the fade set the volume to zero and then
  never raised it. The watchdog only checked that the sound was running, which it
  was. Now the fade runs on plain timers, full volume is forced at 0.7 s whatever
  happens, the soundtrack sync loop puts the volume back to full if anything ever
  lowers it, and the watchdog treats a low volume as silence. Every clip_start,
  clip_audio_start and playback_rate line in the log now records the volume, so
  the next headset run will show in black and white whether the sound played.
  The test suite also runs an approach with the animation clock deliberately
  killed and demands full volume within a second (it failed on 2.7.4).

- THE COCKPIT STAYS WHERE IT IS BETWEEN APPROACHES. Until now the cockpit was
  turned to wherever the participant happened to be looking at the moment they
  pulled the trigger for each approach. A glance to the left at the pull put the
  nose on the left for the whole approach, and nothing could put it back. Now the
  cockpit is turned to the participant's gaze once when they enter VR and once
  more at their first trigger pull in that VR session, and then it is left alone.
  The FLIGHT READY panel now says "Sit square, look straight ahead, then pull the
  trigger for the first approach".

- TWO WAYS TO FIX THE VIEW DURING THE FLIGHT (the participant does these; tell
  them at the briefing):
    HOLD EITHER GRIP for one and a half seconds: the nose is put straight ahead
      of wherever they are looking. A panel says "keep holding the grip"; letting
      go early cancels. Logged as view_recentred.
    PUSH A THUMBSTICK LEFT OR RIGHT: the whole picture slides one degree per
      step. A panel shows the amount. Logged as view_trim with the amount, and
      the amount is written into every playback_rate line. Pointing is scored
      through the current turn, so a trim never corrupts the azimuths.
  Both are only live while a clip is running, between clips, and during the
  takeoff; nothing happens on the readings or the thank-you screen.

- NOSE CALIBRATION BY POINTING, WITH FINE-TUNING. Setup screen, section 4:
    1. "Calibrate": you enter VR on a frame of TC01.
    2. Lay the controller's laser on the propeller spinner and pull the trigger.
       (The pointing direction is used, not the head direction: a laser can be
       laid on the spinner exactly.)
    3. The cockpit turns so the nose it now believes in sits straight ahead,
       under the panel that says NOSE SET AT (so many) DEG. If the real spinner
       is left or right of the panel, push the thumbstick left or right; each
       step slides the picture half a degree. Hold the grip to point again.
    4. Pull the trigger to keep it. The setup screen shows the value, how it was
       pointed and how much you fine-tuned, and the session log carries the same.
  If you take the headset off before the last pull, the previous calibration is
  kept. Under the button there are now four nudge buttons (5 and 1 degree, left
  and right) for a remembered calibration: use them after a session in which the
  log's yaw trim shows the nose sat a little off straight ahead.

- PICTURE STEADYING (Advanced settings, off by default). The clips were exported
  with the camera's own stabilisation off, on purpose, so that the cockpit stays
  fixed in the picture and the pointing frame stays the aircraft's own. The price
  is that the airframe's vibration reaches the eye unfiltered: in the aircraft
  your head shakes with the panel and your eyes steady the world; in VR your head
  is still, so the outside world buzzes. Measured on the takeoff climb and on a
  final-approach window of TC01, the buzz is small in angle, about 0.1 deg RMS
  with peaks near 0.2 deg, mostly side to side at 10 to 15 frames per second,
  which is exactly the kind of motion the eye picks up on the horizon and the
  runway edges.
  What 2.8.0 does about it: for each clip a track can be made offline (from a
  small proxy of the clip) that holds, for every video frame, the tiny rotation
  that cancels only that fast wobble; the aircraft's real motion is kept. The app
  turns the video sphere by that amount as each frame is shown. The clip files
  are not touched, their checksums do not change, and the pointing frame moves by
  less than a fifth of a degree, far inside the pointing error. The panel wobbles
  by that same small amount instead of the world, which is closer to what a pilot
  actually sees. Checked end to end in the app's own rendering: on the takeoff
  window the frame-to-frame wobble fell from 0.13 to 0.04 deg. Tracks live on the
  site as data/motion/<clip>.json; the setup screen's status line says how many
  are there. On 13 September only the TAKEOFF track exists. The twelve approach
  tracks need small proxy copies of the clips, which the PC has to make
  (Claude outputs\_steadying_2026-09-13\README.txt says how). Until a clip has a
  track it plays exactly as recorded, and its clip_start line says so.
  Every clip_start line records whether the picture was steadied.

- VIDEO FRAMES ARE NOW UPLOADED TO THE SPHERE ONCE PER VIDEO FRAME (30 a second)
  instead of once per screen refresh (72 to 90 a second), which is what the 3D
  library does by default. Less work for the headset. xr_entered and every
  playback_rate line record which mode is in use, and there is a fallback to the
  old way if the video frame callback ever stops arriving.

- FINE DETAIL SETTING (Advanced settings): "Smoothed" builds mipmaps for every
  frame, which calms the shimmer on fine edges when the head moves, at the cost of
  extra work per frame. Off by default. Try it once on the headset and read the
  fps and dropped-frame numbers in the playback_rate lines before and after.

- WHAT TO DO ON THE NEXT HEADSET TEST, IN ORDER: reload the page and confirm the
  version line reads 2.8.0; load the clips; sound check; calibrate the nose by
  pointing (section 4) and fine-tune until the spinner sits under the panel; run a
  short test with the takeoff; listen for the engine; try the grip hold and the
  thumbstick; then Reset this headset -> Save a backup, copy the HEADSET_BACKUP zip
  from the Download folder to the computer, and give it to Claude: the log will
  show the volume, the frame rate, dropped frames, the upload mode and the trims.

WHAT CHANGED IN 2.7.3 (a backup you can take before you erase anything)
- WHY. A full reset used to erase the master ledger along with everything else.
  Nobody's own report files were at risk, because those are already saved in the
  headset's Download folder and nothing on the reset screen can reach them. But
  the running master study log on that headset would have started again from
  empty. This closes that.
- NEW BUTTON on the reset screen: "Save a backup of everything on this headset".
  It writes ONE zip into the same Download folder as your report zips, named
  HEADSET_BACKUP_ and the date and time. It copies; it erases nothing.
- WHAT IS IN THE BACKUP:
    00_sessions_summary.csv   one line per stored session: the code, how far it
                              got, approaches finished, when it was last saved,
                              how many events were recorded, and whether its
                              reports had already been saved and locked.
    sessions\                 for each session, the full event log as a
                              spreadsheet (_events.csv) plus the untouched
                              original (_session.json). The spreadsheet carries
                              every field that appears anywhere in that log, so
                              nothing is quietly dropped.
    set_aside_part_sessions\  the same, for part-sessions the app set aside.
    master_log\               the four STUDY_master files and the ledger.
    computer_forms\           the participant forms from the computer station.
    settings.json             report e-mail, researcher, venue, headset name,
                              seat, approach length, nose calibration. The
                              e-mail sending key is deliberately NOT saved.
    erasures.csv              what has been erased from this headset, and when.
    README_READ_ME_FIRST.txt  the above, in plain English, inside the zip.
- THE FULL RESET IS NOW LOCKED until either a backup has been saved, or you tick
  "I already have the files I need". The line above the button tells you how many
  items the headset is holding and when the last backup was taken. If the headset
  is already empty, nothing is required.
- "Clear the leftovers" is not gated. It never touches the master ledger, and it
  never touches a session whose reports were not saved.

WHAT CHANGED IN 2.7.2 (a way to reset the headset)
- WHERE. Home screen -> Researcher -> "Reset this headset". It asks for the
  researcher code before it shows or does anything, so a participant poking
  around the home screen sees nothing.
- WHAT IT SHOWS. Once unlocked it lists everything this headset is holding:
  each stored session with how far it got and when it was last saved, the
  part-sessions that were set aside, the master ledger, the computer forms, the
  locked screen, the nose calibration, the remembered clips folder, and your
  settings.
- THE SAFETY LINE. A session is marked OK when its report files were already
  saved and locked, or when it is a test session. It is marked CARE when its
  reports were NEVER saved from this headset, which means this headset holds
  the only copy of that participant's data. Read that line before you erase
  anything.
- THREE WAYS TO CLEAR, smallest first:
  1. Erase one session. Each line has its own button. Use this for a single
     bad run.
  2. "Clear the leftovers". Erases the part-sessions that were set aside, the
     finished sessions whose reports were already saved, the test sessions, and
     the locked screen. It KEEPS your settings, the nose calibration and the
     remembered clips folder, so the headset is still ready to run. It will not
     touch a session marked CARE. This is the one to use when the home screen
     keeps offering an old unfinished session you no longer want.
  3. "Full reset". Returns the headset to blank: every session, the leftovers,
     the ledger, the computer forms, the locked screen, the calibration, the
     clips folder and the settings. Use it only to start a test from scratch.
     Afterwards, reload the page, pick the clips folder again, run the sound
     check and calibrate the nose.
- NOTHING HAPPENS ON ONE PRESS. Every button on this screen has to be pressed
  twice. The first press changes the button to read "Press again to ...", and
  it goes back to normal on its own after a few seconds if you walk away.
- A RECORD IS KEPT. Each erasure is written to a short record: the code, the
  date and time, how far the session got, and whether its reports had been
  saved. The record survives a full reset on purpose, so you can always say
  what was removed and when. It is at the bottom of the reset screen, and it
  has its own separate button if you ever need to clear it too.

WHAT CHANGED IN 2.7.1 (the app now catches a clip that is the wrong version)
- WHY. On 13 September the headset was found holding the pre-composite versions
  of seven approach clips: the versions with no drone in them. The file names,
  the file count and everything on screen looked right. Only the file size was
  different. Version 2.7.0 would have accepted them by name and shown a green
  ORDER READY. That cannot happen now.
- The app recognises each clip by its exact file size. If it meets a file whose
  size it does not know but whose name matches a study clip, it still loads it
  so you can see what is there, but it now marks it clearly:
    * the clip list shows WRONG instead of OK on that row, and says
      "matched by file name only, not by its size";
    * the box under the clip button shows BAD on that line, with the size it
      expected and the size it found;
    * the order line reads WRONG CLIP VERSION and names the clip;
    * the Ready button stays locked and the line under it says which clip is
      wrong;
    * the saved run-order file marks that clip;
    * the log records it (clip_wrong_version).
  A session cannot be started or resumed until every clip is the finished one.
- WHAT TO DO IF YOU SEE IT. Copy that clip again from its Pass-Card folder on D:
  to the headset's Movies folder, choose "Replace the file in the destination",
  then load the clips again in the headset. The expected sizes for all thirteen
  files are in Project Docs\COPY_TO_HEADSET_AND_BACKUP.txt.
- The order line now reads "each matched by its exact size" only when that is
  actually true of every clip in the run.

WHAT CHANGED IN 2.7.0 (engine sound, and a simpler setup screen)
- ENGINE SOUND FIXED. In the first headset run the approaches were silent. The
  reason: the headset browser will not start a new sound from inside the
  immersive view, because a trigger pull there does not count as a button press.
  The app now uses one sound player for every clip, and that player is started
  (silently) by a button press before the flight, which leaves it allowed to
  play inside the headset. The soundtracks are also downloaded into the headset
  at setup, so a weak Wi-Fi signal during a session cannot silence them.
- NEW STEP 3 ON THE SETUP SCREEN: "Play the engine sound now (4 seconds)".
  Press it and listen. It plays a real approach soundtrack and then says
  SOUND OK, NO SOUND or BLOCKED, and the result goes into the log. The Ready
  button stays locked until the soundtracks are loaded and this check has run.
- If a clip ever plays without its sound, the headset shows "NO ENGINE SOUND,
  please tell the researcher" and the log records it (clip_audio_silent).
- ORDER CHECK: the line at the top of the setup screen now reads ORDER READY
  with a plain sentence, for example "12 approaches in a random order for P07,
  12 different files, each matched by its exact size; takeoff clip loaded". It
  reads NOT READY and names the missing card if a file is absent, and CHECK
  FILES if the same file was matched twice. Everything is decided on the
  headset; the computer is not needed at the venue.
- SIMPLER SCREEN: the setup screen now has five numbered steps and one main
  button in each. The fallback picker sits under "If neither button worked",
  and saving the order and the clip playback test sit under "Extra checks".
- On the participant's closing screen there is now ONE button, the debriefing
  and reveal video. "End the test here" moved into the small Researcher
  section on that screen; it still needs no code.

WHAT CHANGED IN 2.6.5 (after the IRB approved the modification)
- The consent form in use is now Consent Form v3. The consent text on the
  headset screen and the recorded consent reading (recording 12) were changed
  to match it: approaches are described as about two minutes, the takeoff
  segment and its question are described, and the procedures say the interview
  is followed by the debriefing and the video. Nothing else in the recording
  was touched; only that one sentence was replaced.
- After the video of where the drones were, the same screen now offers an
  optional two-minute film, "Behind the scenes", about how the flights were
  recorded and thanks to the people who helped. It is entirely optional,
  it gates nothing, the Finish button stays available, and whether it was
  played is recorded in the log and in the master-log column fieldwork_film
  (watched / started / not played). If the film is not on the site, the offer
  hides itself.

WHAT CHANGED IN 2.6.3- Loading the clips: the setup screen now offers "Choose the folder with the
  clips (all 13 at once)". Press it, open the Movies folder, and confirm the
  folder; every clip is loaded, and nothing has to be ticked one by one. The
  headset then remembers that folder, so for the next participant there is a

  press. If the headset browser does not support this, the buttons either do
  nothing or are hidden, and the old way ("Choose the video files", select all

- 15_flight_ready is no longer listed as a missing optional recording (it
  belongs to the old flow), so the audio line on the setup screen should now
  read READY with nothing after it.

WHAT CHANGED IN 2.6
- The participant leads the session. You set up the headset, press "Ready:
  hand the headset to the participant", and they press the large "Begin the
  experiment" button.
- Consent is signed on the tablet BEFORE the headset goes on. In the headset
  the consent form is optional: they can listen to your 6-minute reading and
  read along, or press "I previously read and signed the consent form, I
  understand it, and I wish to participate". Either way it goes straight to
  the briefing; no taking the headset off, no researcher code. Their choice
  is logged. "I do not wish to take part" is also on that screen (press it
  twice).
- The briefing (new briefing room picture) and the debriefing still have to
  be listened to in full before the button unlocks, and the answers video
  has to play to the end. No code is needed for any of that.
- The takeoff now plays 25 seconds of the takeoff clip, from 20.0 s (end of
  the roll) to 45.0 s (climbing). The nose comes up at about 22.9 s and a
  bird crosses the front windshield at 22.8 to 23.3 s. At the end the flight
  pauses and asks "Did you notice anything unusual during the
  takeoff?". The participant points at YES or NO and pulls the trigger; a YES
  asks them to tell you what they noticed. Their answer and the time they
  took are logged. The next trigger pull goes on to the instructions.
- Each approach is about 2 minutes: playback starts 55 seconds into each
  clip. The clips on the headset are unchanged. The drones, the deviations,
  the threshold crossing and the scoring all come after that point. (Advanced
  settings has "Full clip: about 3 minutes" if you ever need it.)
- The engine sound follows the picture smoothly. 2.5 jumped the sound back
  into step every time it drifted by 0.08 s, which made it choppy on the
  headset; 2.6 speeds it up or slows it down by 3% for a moment instead.
- After the last approach a Thank you screen stays up. It tells the
  participant to take off the headset for a short interview in their own
  words, and that afterwards they can put it back on for a short debriefing
  and the video of where the drones were. It has two buttons:
  "Debriefing and reveal video" (locked until you unlock it after the
  interview, see 2.6.2 above; your debriefing recording plays first, then
  the video, then The End) and
  "End the test" (press twice; goes straight to Thank you / The End). If you
  use End the test, give the debriefing in person: it is logged and the
  master log shows it in the column "closing".
- The setup screen shows this participant's exact run order with the file
  each approach will play, and can save it as a text file.

BEFORE THE PARTICIPANT ARRIVES
On the computer
1. Computer: participant form and wrap-up.
2. Type the code (P01, P02 ...; no names). Date and start time are filled
   in; researcher, venue and headset are remembered from last time.
3. Fill in what you have and save the form.

On the headset
1. Headset: set up and run a session. Type the same code.
2. Loading the clips (step 2 on the screen). First choice: "Use the remembered clips folder" if it is
   there, or "Choose the folder with the clips", which opens the Movies folder
   and takes all 13 in one go. If neither works on this headset, press
   "Choose the video files": open the Movies folder, press and hold the first
   file until it is ticked, tap each of the other 12 (or use Select all if the
   menu offers it), and confirm.
   The order you pick them in does not matter. A web page cannot open the
   headset's files by itself, so this one pick per session is the minimum;
   everything after it is automatic.
3. The box under the button lists every file and what it was matched to
   (by exact file size). All 13 lines should start with OK. The other picker
   ("photos and videos") is only a fallback: it can limit how many files you
   select and can hand over files that do not match.
4. "This participant's run order" now lists the takeoff and approaches 1 to
   12 with the file each will play. Nothing needs arranging or copying.
   "Save this order as a text file" puts it in Downloads if you want it.
5. First time on a headset, and after any browser update: press "Test that
   this headset can open and play every clip". Every line should read OK.
6. Step 3, engine sound: wait for the soundtracks to load, then press "Play
   the engine sound now" and listen. It must say SOUND OK.
7. Nose calibration (once per headset; remembered): Calibrate -> in VR lay the
   laser on the propeller spinner, pull the trigger, fine-tune with the
   thumbstick until the spinner sits under the panel, pull again.
8. The line under the Ready button must read "Everything is ready". Short
   test run (two approaches) is the tick box just above it.
9. Press "Ready: hand the headset to the participant". Put the headset on
   the participant and hand them the controller.

The small "Researcher" sections under the briefing, debriefing and answers
video can unlock a button early if a recording or video will not play
(logged as researcher_unlock), or record that the participant did that step
outside the headset (logged as briefing_outside_headset and so on). The End
screen's Researcher section opens the results.

consent_in_headset (listened in full / skipped: previously read and signed /

AFTER THE PARTICIPANT LEAVES
On the headset
1. On The End screen open "Researcher", type the code, press "Open the
   researcher results".
2. Press "1 Save all report files". One zip lands in the headset's
   Downloads folder (Files app -> Downloads). It now also holds
   Pxx_session.json, the complete session the computer reads.
3. Type the code and press "2 Lock". The headset saves the zip again and
   locks until the code is entered for the next participant. The headset no
   longer opens a mail draft; e-mail is sent from the computer. (If an
   e-mail key is set on the headset it still sends, so leave the key blank
   there to avoid getting two e-mails.)
4. Copy the zip to the computer: plug the headset in with the USB cable,
   allow file access in the headset, then in File Explorer open
   Quest 3 -> Internal shared storage -> Download.

On the computer
1. Computer: participant form and wrap-up.
2. "Choose the headset's report zip" and pick the zip. The line under it
   says whose session it is, how many approaches finished, and whether the
   saved form for that code was found (it is loaded into the form).
3. Check or finish the form. Press "Join with the form and open the
   reports". The form must have the same code as the headset file (a test
   run P01_TEST joins with the form P01).
4. Press "1 Save all report files" (zip in the computer's Downloads), type
   the code, press "2 Lock on this computer: master log and e-mail". The
   participant is added to this computer's master log and the report is
   e-mailed (with the Web3Forms key entered under Station settings; without
   it a mail draft opens and you attach the zip by hand). The station comes
   back with the participant marked "locked" in the list.
5. "Save the master study log" gives the up-to-date STUDY_master_log files
   for every participant locked on this computer (test runs left out).

Older zips without Pxx_session.json (from 2.4.1, or the "Save the last
participant's files again" button on the locked headset) still import; the
app rebuilds the session from Pxx_results.json.

IF THE HEADSET CRASHES, THE BROWSER CLOSES, OR THE PAGE RELOADS
Every step is saved as it happens. Open the app again on the headset: the
home screen shows "Continue an interrupted session: P07, ..." with the step
it stopped at (the consent reading, the signature step, the briefing, the
takeoff, approach N, the interview pause, the debriefing, the answers
video). Press it, and on the setup screen press Continue.
- Stopped before or during the flight (consent, signature, briefing,
  takeoff, approaches): choose the 13 clips again first; Continue says so
  if they are missing. A cut-off approach starts again from its beginning
  (logged as a replay); finished approaches are never repeated. A cut-off
  takeoff plays again from the start. A cut-off consent reading starts
  again from the Welcome screen.
- Stopped after the approaches (interview pause, debriefing, answers
  video): no clips needed; it goes straight back to that screen.
Pressing Begin instead of Continue does not overwrite anything: the old
session is moved to an archive entry and a fresh one starts.
