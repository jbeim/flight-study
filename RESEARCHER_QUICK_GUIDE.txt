N41533 DETECTION STUDY APP, VERSION 2.5.1: RESEARCHER QUICK GUIDE
(11 September 2026)

WHAT CHANGED IN 2.5, IN ONE PARAGRAPH
The participant now goes through every step in the headset by pressing
buttons: Welcome, the consent form with your recorded reading, a pause to
sign on the tablet, the briefing in the pilot lounge with your recorded
reading, a one-minute takeoff, the approach instructions, the twelve
approaches, a pause for the interview, the debriefing in the sunset lounge
with your recorded reading, the "where the drones were" video, and The End.
You do the typing on the computer: the participant form before the session,
and the wrap-up (reports, master log, e-mail) after it. On the headset you
only enter the code, load the clips, check calibration, and press Begin.
The narration pieces 01 (welcome and safety) and 02 (briefing) are no longer
played; the on-screen Welcome and the briefing reading replace them.

THE TWO PLACES THE APP RUNS
  Computer   open jbeim.github.io/flight-study in Chrome or Edge ->
             "Computer: participant form and wrap-up".
             Keep using the same browser on the same computer: the forms and
             the master log are stored in that browser.
  Headset    open the same address in the headset browser ->
             "Headset: set up and run a session".

BEFORE THE PARTICIPANT ARRIVES
On the computer
1. Computer: participant form and wrap-up.
2. Type the code (P01, P02 ...; no names). Date and start time are filled
   in; researcher, venue and headset are remembered from last time.
3. Fill in what you have (certificate, instructor, hours, recent hours,
   vision correction, VR experience, prior drone sighting, the three
   consent answers, notes). Anything left blank can be filled in later,
   up to the moment you lock.
4. Save the form. It appears in the "Saved forms" list as "form only".

On the headset
1. Headset: set up and run a session.
2. Type the same code.
3. Choose the video files: press the button, then select all 13 files in
   the Movies folder (the 12 approaches and the takeoff). The app now
   recognizes each clip by its exact file size, so the names the headset's
   file picker shows do not matter. Under the button a box lists every file
   the picker handed over and what the app matched it to. If the box says
   the picker returned no files, or some are "not one of the study clips",
   use "Still missing some? Choose them from all files instead".
4. First time on a headset, and after any browser update: press "Test that
   this headset can open and play every clip". It opens each 8K file, jumps
   into it and waits for a picture. Every line should read OK. If any line
   says FAILED, copy what it says to Claude before running a participant.
5. Nose calibration (once per headset; remembered).
6. The line under Begin says what is still missing. It must read
   "Everything is ready". Short test run (two approaches, _TEST on every
   file) is the tick box just above Begin. The e-mail key, the seat
   position and the optional participant information on the headset are
   under "Advanced settings".
7. Press Begin. The Welcome screen appears. Hand over the headset and the
   controller.

WHAT THE PARTICIPANT SEES (and what you do)
 1 Welcome              participant presses Start
 2 Consent form         your recording (12_consent_reading) starts; the text
                        scrolls with the thumbstick. The "I have listened to
                        and read the consent form" button stays locked until
                        the recording ends.
 3 Take off the headset the participant signs on the tablet with you. Open
                        "Researcher", type the code, press "Consent form
                        signed on the tablet: continue". If the person
                        decides not to take part: type the code and press
                        "The participant decided not to take part" twice.
                        Nothing further is recorded; their screen thanks
                        them. Hand the headset back.
 4 Briefing             pilot lounge picture, your recording
                        (11_prebriefing_reading), flight conditions, call
                        sign, the NOTAMs, what to report, tolerances, and
                        how the session runs. Ask your comprehension
                        question here if you use one. The button unlocks
                        when the recording ends; pressing it goes into the
                        cockpit view.
 5 Takeoff              about one minute from the takeoff roll to the climb,
                        with engine sound. Nothing is scored.
 6 "Test approaches next" your recordings 03 (instructions) and 04 (ready)
                        play in the cockpit view, then "READY TO BEGIN: pull
                        the trigger for the first approach".
 7 Twelve approaches    as before: trigger = I see it, point and trigger =
                        lock in, red panel behind the seat = pressed by
                        mistake, green panel = replay. Break after
                        approach 6 (headset off, questionnaire, Continue).
 8 All approaches complete  headset off. Do the interview. Then open
                        "Researcher", type the code, press "Interview
                        finished: go to the debriefing", hand the headset
                        back.
 9 Debriefing           sunset lounge picture, your recording
                        (13_debriefing_reading); the button unlocks when it
                        ends.
10 Where the drones were  the answers video; Finish unlocks when it ends.
11 Thank you / The End  participant takes off the headset.

LOCKED BUTTONS AND THE RESEARCHER CODE
Every "Researcher" section needs the code. On the three readings and the
answers video you can unlock the button early (for example if a recording
will not play). Every early unlock is written to the log as
researcher_unlock and counted in the master-log column researcher_unlocks.

AFTER THE PARTICIPANT LEAVES
On the headset
1. On The End screen open "Researcher", type the code, press "Open the
   researcher results".
2. Press "1 Save all report files". One zip lands in the headset's
   Downloads folder (Files app -> Downloads). It now also holds
   Pxx_session.json, the complete session the computer reads.
3. Type the code and press "2 Lock". The headset saves the zip again and
   locks until the code is entered for the next participant. The headset no
   longer opens a mail draft; e-mail is sent from the computer. (If an
   e-mail key is set on the headset it still sends, so leave the key blank
   there to avoid getting two e-mails.)
4. Copy the zip to the computer: plug the headset in with the USB cable,
   allow file access in the headset, then in File Explorer open
   Quest 3 -> Internal shared storage -> Download.

On the computer
1. Computer: participant form and wrap-up.
2. "Choose the headset's report zip" and pick the zip. The line under it
   says whose session it is, how many approaches finished, and whether the
   saved form for that code was found (it is loaded into the form).
3. Check or finish the form. Press "Join with the form and open the
   reports". The form must have the same code as the headset file (a test
   run P01_TEST joins with the form P01).
4. Press "1 Save all report files" (zip in the computer's Downloads), type
   the code, press "2 Lock on this computer: master log and e-mail". The
   participant is added to this computer's master log and the report is
   e-mailed (with the Web3Forms key entered under Station settings; without
   it a mail draft opens and you attach the zip by hand). The station comes
   back with the participant marked "locked" in the list.
5. "Save the master study log" gives the up-to-date STUDY_master_log files
   for every participant locked on this computer (test runs left out).

Older zips without Pxx_session.json (from 2.4.1, or the "Save the last
participant's files again" button on the locked headset) still import; the
app rebuilds the session from Pxx_results.json.

IF THE HEADSET CRASHES, THE BROWSER CLOSES, OR THE PAGE RELOADS
Every step is saved as it happens. Open the app again on the headset: the
home screen shows "Continue an interrupted session: P07, ..." with the step
it stopped at (the consent reading, the signature step, the briefing, the
takeoff, approach N, the interview pause, the debriefing, the answers
video). Press it, and on the setup screen press Continue.
- Stopped before or during the flight (consent, signature, briefing,
  takeoff, approaches): choose the 13 clips again first; Continue says so
  if they are missing. A cut-off approach starts again from its beginning
  (logged as a replay); finished approaches are never repeated. A cut-off
  takeoff plays again from the start. A cut-off consent reading starts
  again from the Welcome screen.
- Stopped after the approaches (interview pause, debriefing, answers
  video): no clips needed; it goes straight back to that screen.
Pressing Begin instead of Continue does not overwrite anything: the old
session is moved to an archive entry and a fresh one starts.

RESUME SCREEN: RESEARCHER OPTIONS (code required)
Unchanged: Restart this approach, Skip this approach, End the session here
(now goes to the interview pause, then the debriefing and The End as usual).

NEW COLUMNS IN THE PARTICIPANT ROW AND THE MASTER LOG (142 columns now)
consent_audio, consent_followup (from the form), headset_first_on (Start on
the Welcome screen), consent_reading_min, consent_signed_local,
consent_declined, briefing_min, takeoff (played / did not open / skipped
(test run) / started, not finished), ready_instructions_heard,
interview_pause_min, debrief_min, answers_video_watched (1, started, 0),
the_end_reached, researcher_unlocks. The event log has a new "stage" column
for resumes. Everything else (scoring, per-card columns, issues, seat
offset, order seed) is unchanged from 2.4.1.

begin (Short test run no longer required).
