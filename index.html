Nothing leaves this page. The clip is read locally and never uploaded.

Welcome, and thank you for taking part.

You will watch a short cockpit flight in full 360. Look anywhere you like, the way you would scan for traffic in a real aircraft.

If you see a drone, press the trigger right away; the flight pauses. Point the controller at where you saw it and press again to lock it in. The flight then continues.

You may do this as many times as you see something. If you press by mistake, turn around and use the red panel behind you: it will be noted, and the flight continues. The flight ends on its own.

When the spoken instructions finish, a button appears here.
